# Customer Support

## Response times

Every customer message receives a first reply within four working hours. Urgent requests, marked with the red flag in the ticket system, are answered within one hour.

## Tone and language

Write short, clear sentences and answer in the customer's language. Never promise a date you are not sure about: say when you will update the customer instead.

## Escalation to the manager

If a customer is angry, threatens to cancel, or asks for something you cannot decide, hand the ticket to your manager. Tell the manager who the customer is, what has already been said, and when the customer expects to be called back. Never leave the customer waiting without news for more than one working day.

## Customer refunds

A customer can ask for a refund within 14 days of the purchase. The support agent checks the order and approves refunds up to 100 euros; larger amounts need the manager. The money is returned by the same payment method within five working days.

## Closing a ticket

Close a ticket only after the customer has confirmed that the problem is solved, or after three days without a reply.
