# Expense Reimbursements

## Business trips

Travel for work must be authorized by your manager before you book. Trains and economy-class flights are reimbursed in full. Taxis are reimbursed only when public transport is not available.

## Using your own car

If you use your own car for a business trip, you are reimbursed 0.35 euros per kilometer. Write the departure, the destination and the kilometers on form RS-12, and attach it to the expense report. Tolls and parking are reimbursed on presentation of the receipt.

## Meals and lodging

Meals during a business trip are reimbursed up to 30 euros per day. Lodging is reimbursed up to 120 euros per night, breakfast included. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Receipts and deadlines

Every expense needs its receipt. Submit the expense report within 30 days of the expense; older expenses are paid only with the written approval of the finance office. If you lose a receipt, fill in a declaration of loss and ask your manager to sign it.
