# Vacation and Leave

## Vacation days

Every employee has 26 days of vacation per year, counted in working days. Unused days can be carried over until the end of March of the following year; after that they are lost.

## How to request vacation

Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 15 days before the start date. Your manager approves or declines the request within five working days. Periods of more than ten consecutive days need the approval of the department head.

## Illness

If you are sick, notify your manager by 9:00 AM on the first day of absence, by phone or message. The medical certificate must be sent to HR within two days. If the illness continues, send a new certificate for each extension.

## Personal leave

Employees can take up to eight hours of paid personal leave per month for medical appointments or official errands. Request it in the HR portal at least two days in advance, except in emergencies.
