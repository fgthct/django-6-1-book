# Guide for New Hires

## The first day

On your first day come to the office at 9:30. The reception desk will give you your badge, and the IT team will give you your laptop and your accounts. In the afternoon your manager presents the team and the projects you will work on.

## Reference people

For questions about your contract and your payslip, write to the HR office. For laptop and account problems, call the IT desk. For anything else, your first reference is the colleague assigned to you as a buddy for the first three months.

## Working from home

After the first month you can work from home two days a week, agreed with your manager. On those days you are expected to be available during core hours, from 10:00 to 16:00, and to connect through the company VPN.

## Meetings and communication

The whole company meets on the first Monday of the month at 10:00. Daily questions go through the team chat; email is for customers and formal communications.

## Training

Every employee has 20 hours of paid training per year. Choose a course with your manager and register it in the HR portal.
