# Information Security

## Passwords and access

Use the company password manager and never reuse a password. Every account must have two-factor authentication. If you lose the phone that holds the second factor, call the IT desk: they will reset it after checking your identity.

## Laptops

Company laptops have an encrypted disk and lock themselves after five minutes of inactivity. Never leave a laptop in a car or unattended in a public place. Install software only from the company catalog.

## Incidents

If your computer is lost or stolen, call the security desk immediately at extension 4100, at any hour, and then tell your manager. The same number is for suspicious emails that you have already opened. Do not wait until the next morning: the sooner the account is blocked, the smaller the damage.

## Working outside the office

When you work from home, connect through the company VPN and keep your home router updated. Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
